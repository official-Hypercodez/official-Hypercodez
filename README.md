# 🌍 Knowzup

### Social Knowledge Sharing & Learning Platform

🔗 **Website:** https://knowzup.rf.gd

Knowzup is designed to make knowledge sharing and learning more flexible.

Users can:

- Ask questions
- Share knowledge
- Create educational and general content
- Post text, images, and videos
- Reply using different media formats
- Search and discover knowledge
- Find unanswered questions
- Follow useful users and content
- Build reputation through their contributions

The idea is simple:

> **Knowledge doesn't always fit into one format.**

A question may need a written explanation.

A concept may be easier to understand with an image.

A tutorial or demonstration may work better as a video.

Knowzup is designed to support these different forms of communication in one platform.
